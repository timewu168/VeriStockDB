VeriStockDB Official Data Source URL Reference

Language: English
Chinese version: ../URL.txt

This file summarizes the accepted official sources used by VeriStockDB. Always verify official endpoint behavior before changing downloader, parser, or importer logic.

1. Trading Days

TWSE monthly trading-day source:
https://www.twse.com.tw/rwd/zh/afterTrading/FMTQIK?date={date_yyyymmdd}&response=json

Parameter:
- {date_yyyymmdd}: Gregorian date in YYYYMMDD, usually the first day of the month.

TPEX fallback monthly market index source:
https://www.tpex.org.tw/www/zh-tw/afterTrading/tradingIndex?date={date_url}&id=&response=json

Parameter:
- {date_url}: URL-encoded Gregorian date YYYY%2FMM%2FDD, usually the first day of the month.

2. Daily Close

TWSE:
https://www.twse.com.tw/rwd/zh/afterTrading/MI_INDEX?response=csv&date={sii_date}&type=ALLBUT0999NOTIND

TPEX:
https://www.tpex.org.tw/www/zh-tw/afterTrading/dailyQuotes?date={otc_date_url}&id=&response=csv

Parameters:
- {sii_date}: YYYYMMDD.
- {otc_date_url}: YYYY%2FMM%2FDD.

3. Legal Investors

TWSE:
https://www.twse.com.tw/rwd/zh/fund/T86?date={sii_date}&selectType=ALLBUT0999&response=csv

TPEX:
https://www.tpex.org.tw/www/zh-tw/insti/dailyTrade?type=Daily&sect=EW&date={otc_date_url}&id=&response=csv

Canonical SQLite scope:
- TWSE from 2012-05-04.
- TPEX from 2007-04-23.

4. Margin Trading

TWSE:
https://www.twse.com.tw/rwd/zh/marginTrading/MI_MARGN?response=csv&date={sii_date}&selectType=ALL

TPEX accepted canonical source:
https://www.tpex.org.tw/www/zh-tw/margin/balance?date={otc_date_url}&id=&response=csv

Canonical SQLite scope:
- TWSE from 2001-01-02.
- TPEX from 2008-09-30.

5. Day Trading

TWSE:
https://www.twse.com.tw/rwd/zh/dayTrading/TWTB4U?response=csv&date={sii_date}&selectType=All

TPEX:
https://www.tpex.org.tw/www/zh-tw/intraday/stat?type=Daily&date={otc_date_url}&id=&response=csv

Canonical SQLite scope:
- From 2014-01-06.

6. Monthly Revenue

TWSE:
https://mopsov.twse.com.tw/server-java/FileDownLoad?step=9&functionName=show_file2&filePath=%2Ft21%2Fsii%2F&fileName=t21sc03_{roc_month}.csv

TPEX:
https://mopsov.twse.com.tw/server-java/FileDownLoad?step=9&functionName=show_file2&filePath=%2Ft21%2Fotc%2F&fileName=t21sc03_{roc_month}.csv

Parameter:
- {roc_month}: ROCYear_Month, for example 115_6.

Important header:
- The download request must include Referer: https://mops.twse.com.tw/mops/web/t21sc03_bootstrap

Canonical SQLite scope:
- From 2013-01.

7. Attention Notices

TWSE:
https://www.twse.com.tw/rwd/zh/announcement/notice?response=csv&startDate={start_date_sii}&endDate={end_date_sii}&stockNo=&sortKind=STKNO&querytype=1&selectType=

TPEX:
https://www.tpex.org.tw/www/zh-tw/bulletin/attention?startDate={start_date_otc}&endDate={end_date_otc}&code=&cate=&type=all&order=date&id=&response=csv

8. Disposal Notices

TWSE:
https://www.twse.com.tw/rwd/zh/announcement/punish?response=csv&startDate={start_date_sii}&endDate={end_date_sii}&stockNo=&sortKind=DATE&querytype=&selectType=&proceType=&remarkType=

TPEX:
https://www.tpex.org.tw/www/zh-tw/bulletin/disposal?startDate={start_date_otc}&endDate={end_date_otc}&code=&cate=&type=all&reason=-1&measure=-1&order=date&id=&response=csv

Operational note:
- Disposal update should query beyond the latest accepted announcement date to capture official records that appear only when the endpoint date window is extended.

9. Close Monthly Reconciliation

TWSE stock monthly JSON:
https://www.twse.com.tw/exchangeReport/STOCK_DAY?response=json&date={yyyymm01}&stockNo={stock_id}

TPEX stock monthly JSON:
https://www.tpex.org.tw/www/zh-tw/afterTrading/tradingStock?date={yyyy_mm_01_url}&code={stock_id}&response=json

Use:
- Reconciliation compares close and volume only.
- It must not overwrite canonical daily_close rows automatically.
